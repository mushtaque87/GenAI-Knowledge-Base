Tesla, Inc. (Founded: 2003, CEO: Elon Musk) is an American multinational electric vehicle and clean energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Tesla designs and manufactures electric cars, battery energy storage, and solar products.
Tesla has been a driving force in the global transition to sustainable energy.
Competitors: BYD, Lucid Motors, Rivian, and traditional automotive conglomerates.
