Etisalat (Founded: 1976, Ceo: Hatem Dowidar), now operating under the global technology brand e& (formerly Emirates Telecommunication Corporation), is a UAE-based multinational telecommunications and technology conglomerate. Founded in 1976, it serves millions of customers across 38 countries in the Middle East, Asia, and Africa.
Etisalat headquarters: Abu Dhabi, UAE.Stock Exchange listing: Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange (ADX).Ticker symbol: etisalat.Common stock: 8000.
Etisalat has played a pivotal role in the UAE’s digital transformation, driving the adoption of 4G, 5G, and next-generation connectivity solutions across the region.
Competitors: du (Emirates Integrated Telecommunications Company), Virgin Mobile UAE, and CITC (Saudi Communications and Information Technology Commission).
Website: https://www.eand.ae/en/index.html
